Simple Loot Council — Feature Guide

By Laxx

Simple Loot Council (SLC) runs loot council voting for WoW retail 12.1.5 (Midnight) and WoW: Forever, synced live across every raider's client. Retail has no Master Looter, so the council votes on items sitting in a raider's bags during the four-hour trade window; the Loot Master awards, and the holder trades the item across.

SLC runs in raids. Every participant needs the addon; players without it still show up in the council table and can answer by whisper.

Getting started

  1. Install it from an addon site: CurseForge, Wago or WoWInterface (retail only there), through your addon manager or by downloading the zip. The same build is on GitHub. By hand, unzip the SimpleLootCouncil folder into World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns. With WowUp: Get Addons → Install from URL → paste https://github.com/SimpleLootCouncil/SimpleLootCouncil, and WowUp keeps it updated.
  2. /reload in game. No client restart is needed, even for a first install.
  3. Type /slc to open settings, or click the minimap button. /glc works as an alias for every command.
  4. The group leader is the Loot Master by default. Promote someone else with /slc lootmaster Name-Realm.
  5. Add your council: /slc council add Name-Realm, or tick guild ranks under Council by guild rank in settings.
  6. Try it solo first: /slc test 6 opens a simulated run with six items so you can see every window.
If WowUp says Addon Installation Failed, go to Options → Addons and clear the GitHub Personal Access Token field, then try again.

No library dependencies. Optional integrations: WowUtils, Guilds of WoW or wowaudit (wishlist and sim columns), SimulationCraft (profile export), and RCLootCouncil / Loothing CSV exports (history import). Disable RCLootCouncil's own looting if you run the SLC funnel - the two conflict.

How a loot run works

A run goes from the boss kill to a completed trade in seven steps, and every step is shared live with the group.

  1. Opt in. On entering a raid, the Loot Master is asked "Use Simple Loot Council for this raid?". Raiders are then asked to join the Loot Master's Loot Council, and answer Join & Auto-Pass or Roll Normally. Nobody is asked anything until the Loot Master says yes, and nothing is asked in battlegrounds or arenas. The Loot Master can also switch it with /slc on and /slc off, or by clicking the Active tag.
  2. Collect. The Loot Master rolls Need and participating raiders pass, so loot lands in one pair of bags. How far this reaches is the Loot funnel in which groups? setting: Guild groups by default, so you are asked before your loot is passed outside your guild; All groups; or Never, in which case raiders keep what they win and the council still votes on it.
  3. Stage. On a boss kill the Loot Master's window opens. Items are queued privately on the Loot queue tab (/slc queue), from their own bags or, if enabled, council members' bags. Staging opens nothing on anyone else's screen. If Council members can manage the loot queue allows it, council members can queue items too. Optionally, Automatically start session on boss kill starts a session with the drops the Loot Master's roll won, and new drops join an open session.
  4. Start. Start run sends the items to the group. Every raider gets the Loot response window; the council gets the Loot council table. Items can be added to a run in progress.
  5. Respond. Raiders pick a response (Mainspec, Offspec and Transmog by default, all configurable), add an optional note, or pass. Unusable items can auto-pass.
  6. Vote and award. The council votes; the Loot Master awards. Awards can go to a player or "for a reason" (disenchant, guild bank, free). Award, Re-award and Undo act at once, with no popup.
  7. Trade. Whoever holds the item sees it in Loot to Trade. Opening a trade with the winner fills the window automatically; you press Trade. The award is marked delivered only once the item has actually changed hands.

The boss each item dropped from is recorded automatically, from the kill or from the Adventure Guide. The game blocks addon communication during boss encounters and Mythic+, so loot seen mid-fight reaches the group when the pull ends; normal combat is fine.

For the council

The Loot council table shows every candidate for every item, and votes appear on every council member's table as they are cast. Reopen it any time with /slc vote (/slc v). Right-click a column header to hide or show columns.

Column / featureWhat it shows

The table sorts by Response, and casting a vote never moves a row. The response countdown sits in the title band.

Hover a candidate for the council tooltip: their most recent loot, who else has won this item, alt/main link, guild note, officer note (hold Shift, if you can see it), and their equipped item with sockets and average item level.

Voting rules are the Loot Master's and apply to the whole group, so a vote means the same thing on every screen:

  • Council may vote for themselves (on by default)
  • Allow voting for several candidates per item (on)
  • Hide vote counts until you have voted, to stop bandwagoning (off)

Rolls: right-click a row or item to ask one player, everyone on a response, or the whole raid to roll; or generate rolls instantly, for one player or one item. Clear rolls from the same menu. /slc roll ask, /slc roll random, /slc roll clear do the same for every unawarded item.

WoW: Forever

The same download runs on WoW: Forever as well as retail. A few things differ there:

  • No specs, bonus rolls, wishlist or upgrade-track columns: Forever has none of them, so SLC leaves them out. Auto-pass judges items by what your class can use.
  • The Master Looter loot method is available. When the group uses it, the Master Looter is SLC's Loot Master and nobody's rolls are passed. Auto-loot to bags as Master Looter sends a boss's whole corpse to their bags for the council, and in a raid any trash item at the loot threshold too.
  • Names are typed without a realm - /slc council add Ash Thornwood, not Name-Realm. Forever names are two words and unique across the game.
  • New profiles start with Mainspec and Offspec buttons; add a Transmog button if your guild wants one.
  • WowUp finds Forever installs from version 2.24.0.

Languages

SLC follows your game client's language and is translated into German, French, Spanish (Spain), Latin American Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Not every translation has been checked by a native speaker yet, so some wording may be off: corrections and new translators are welcome in the Discord. Item names, character names and the buttons your group defined are never translated.

Reliability

A run survives reloads, disconnects and loading screens on any client, including the Loot Master's.

  • Reloads. A raider, council member or Loot Master who /reloads mid-run keeps the session: responses, notes, votes, rolls and coins intact. A promoted Loot Master keeps the role too.
  • Leaving by mistake. A raider or council member who leaves or is removed during a session gets it back when invited again, and can answer and vote as before.
  • Nothing owed is forgotten. A reload never loses who you owe what.
  • Delivery is checked against your bags, not assumed. An item that was equipped, sold, or traded to the wrong player is flagged, and the Loot Master is told.
  • Loading screens and crowd control. The game closes addon windows on every zone change, fear or mind control; SLC reopens the ones you did not close yourself.
  • Boss encounters and Mythic+. The game blocks addon communication there. Whatever happens during the pull reaches the group when it ends, and nothing is lost.
  • Other players can't break your run. A damaged or out-of-date copy of SLC in the group cannot break your run, history or rolls.
  • Light on resources. Closed windows and an idle addon cost almost nothing, and history and snapshots are stored compactly.
  • Both editions. If you also run the EllesmereUI edition, the two share one set of saved data and neither loses a deletion or an edit.

Testing and reporting bugs

Most of SLC can be tried solo; a real bug report needs the log from every client involved. Questions and feedback are welcome in the Discord, and bugs go in the issue tracker.

Solo checks

  • /slc test 6 - a simulated run with six classic-raid items. Open every window, respond, vote, award.
  • /slc test [item link] from heroic <boss> - test a specific item, attributed to a boss and difficulty.
  • /slc fakecoins #2 - fake a bonus roll on item 2 to see the Bonus rolls tag. No coin is spent and nothing reaches history.
  • /slc activetest (/slc at) - allow a real session solo or in a party, with nothing automatic.
  • /slc ping - checks that addon messages get there and back.
  • /slc history, /slc team, /slc queue, /slc trades - browse each window.
  • Test awards from /slc test never reach your real history.

With a friend: start a test run, then /reload one client mid-run and check that everything comes back.

Before testing, run /console scriptErrors 1 so Lua errors show on screen. BugSack and BugGrabber help too. The log records only warnings and errors by default; for a bug that needs more detail, type /slc log level verbose on every client involved before you reproduce it, and /slc log level quiet afterwards.

When something goes wrong, send:

  1. /slc log from every affected player, copied out of the window.
  2. /slc dump from the Loot Master.
  3. What you clicked, and what you expected to happen.

The log is timestamped and survives reloads, so comparing the Loot Master's with a raider's usually shows what went wrong. After an update, /reload twice before reading the log: the first reload still shows the old build's state.
